## What we build

BTB Finance builds open-source tools for liquidity providers and DeFi users.

- **LP simulator** – model fees, impermanent loss and price ranges before you deposit.
- **Position management** – add, rebalance and manage concentrated liquidity on Uniswap V3 and V4, PancakeSwap and Aerodrome.
- **Pool research** – compare APR and TVL across pools.
- **Multichain swaps** – trade across supported chains from a single interface.
- **Points and revenue sharing** – activity in the app earns points, and BTB shares revenue with users.
